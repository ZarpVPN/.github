# 🤝 Zarp Community Guidelines

Welcome to the Zarp Community! 🚀

Zarp is an independent open-source project maintained on a best-effort basis.

Our community is a place to ask questions, share ideas, and help each other.

## ❤️ Be Respectful

- Treat others with respect.
- No harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Keep discussions constructive and relevant.
- Avoid spam, scams, and misleading content.

## 💬 Community Discussions

- **Q&A** - Ask questions and help others.
- **Ideas** - Suggest features and improvements.
- **General** - Share feedback and experiences.
- **Polls** - Vote on future improvements.
- **Announcements** - Official project updates.

Community members are encouraged to help one another.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

Never share passwords, private keys, VPN credentials, access tokens, or sensitive configuration files.

Remove personal information and secrets from screenshots and logs before posting.

## 🚨 Reporting Abuse

If you encounter spam, harassment, or content that violates GitHub's policies, use GitHub's built-in **Report content** feature to report it to GitHub Support.

Learn more: [Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## 🛡️ Moderation

Zarp is a community-driven project without a dedicated moderation or support team.

Project maintainers may remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict participation when necessary.

Moderation and support are provided on a **best-effort basis**, without guaranteed response times.

## ❤️ Thank You!

Be kind, help others when you can, and enjoy being part of the Zarp Community!
